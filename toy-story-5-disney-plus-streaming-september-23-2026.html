Toy Story 5 Is Now Streaming on Disney+: What the New Movie Changes

Latest news and confirmed details · September 24, 2026

HomeAnimeMoviesTechSpaceGaming

Toy Story 5 has arrived on Disney+, giving audiences another chance to return to the world of Woody, Buzz Lightyear, Jessie and their friends. Disney's official streaming guide confirms that the fifth movie began streaming on September 23, 2026, after its theatrical run.

The new sequel keeps the familiar characters but gives them a noticeably different problem. Instead of simply dealing with the changing life of a child, the toys now face a new kind of competition from technology. That idea sits at the center of the film's marketing and its main story hook.

Toy Story meets tech

The central conflict begins when a new tablet device called Lilypad enters Bonnie's home. Lilypad has its own ideas about what is best for Bonnie, putting the traditional role of toys in the household under pressure. Woody, Buzz, Jessie and the other toys therefore have to deal with an opponent that is not another toy but a piece of technology competing for the same attention.

That setup gives Toy Story 5 an easy way to connect its classic themes with a modern household. The earlier movies often focused on friendship, growing up and the emotional meaning of objects. The fifth chapter places those ideas against a world where screens and connected devices can become an important part of how children play and interact.

Returning voices and new characters

The film brings back the voices associated with its central characters. Tom Hanks returns as Woody, Tim Allen as Buzz Lightyear and Joan Cusack as Jessie. Greta Lee voices Lilypad, the tablet character at the center of the new conflict.

The cast also includes Conan O'Brien and Tony Hale, adding more recognizable voices to the animated ensemble. The new characters help the sequel refresh the franchise without abandoning the characters that have defined it for decades.

Runtime and rating

Disney+ lists Toy Story 5 with a runtime of 1 hour and 47 minutes and a PG rating. The movie is directed by Andrew Stanton, who is also credited as a writer and has a long history with Pixar storytelling.

The length keeps the film relatively compact for a major animated feature while leaving enough room for the toys to explore another full adventure. For viewers using Disney+ as their first stop, the movie is available alongside the previous Toy Story films and related shorts and specials.

Why the technology theme stands out

The Toy Story franchise has always been about toys existing in a world where children grow and their interests change. Toy Story 5 gives that idea a contemporary update. Lilypad represents a device designed to attract attention in a way that toys traditionally did through imagination and physical play.

This does not mean the movie abandons the emotional foundation of the series. Instead, the conflict creates another way to ask what makes play meaningful. Woody and the other toys have to prove their value in a household where technology can offer entertainment, information and interaction from one screen.

Where to watch Toy Story 5

Disney's official listing confirms that Toy Story 5 is now streaming on Disney+ from September 23, 2026. Availability, plans and subscription pricing can vary by country, but the release date is confirmed by Disney's platform listings.

For long-time fans, the fifth movie continues a franchise that has spent more than three decades returning to the secret lives of toys. For newer viewers, it also provides an accessible modern entry point built around a simple question: what happens when toys have to compete with technology for a child's attention?
