The Weight is a 2026 survival thriller directed by Padraic McKinley and starring Ethan Hawke, Russell Crowe and Julia Jones. The film blends a prison escape proposition with a dangerous journey through the wilderness.

🎬 The story

Ethan Hawke plays Samuel Murphy, who is separated from his daughter and sent to a brutal prison. Warden Clancy, played by Russell Crowe, offers him a chance at freedom if he joins a crew tasked with smuggling gold from a remote mine.

👥 Cast

The principal cast includes Ethan Hawke, Russell Crowe, Julia Jones, Austin Amelio and Avi Nash. The film's production credits list Fields Entertainment and augenschein Filmproduktion, with Capelight Pictures as distributor.

⏱️ Runtime

The film runs for approximately 1 hour 55 minutes, according to current Apple TV listing information.

📅 Release

The Weight is listed for theatrical release in September 2026, with some cinemas scheduling opening shows from September 18. Local release dates can vary by territory.
