Resident Evil is returning to cinemas in 2026 with a new story rather than a direct continuation of the earlier film series. Sony Pictures says the film comes from filmmaker Zach Cregger, known for Weapons and Barbarian.

🎬 Release date

Sony Pictures lists September 18, 2026 as the theatrical release date. The studio describes the film as an all-new reinvention of the franchise.

👥 Cast

The announced cast includes Austin Abrams, Zach Cherry, Kali Reis and Paul Walter Hauser. Abrams plays Bryan, a medical courier caught in a dangerous night of escalating chaos.

🧟 What is the story about?

According to Sony's official synopsis, Bryan finds himself in a race for survival after a seemingly ordinary delivery turns into a horrifying night. The story is framed as a fresh entry point into the Resident Evil universe.

🎥 What makes this version different?

Instead of relying on a simple remake of one game or a continuation of the previous movie continuity, the new film is being presented as a fresh take. That gives the filmmakers room to build a distinct horror-thriller identity while retaining the franchise's survival-horror roots.

📅 Why September matters

The September release places Resident Evil among a busy run of 2026 theatrical releases. Moviegoers can expect the film to compete for attention with several major fall titles arriving around the same period.
