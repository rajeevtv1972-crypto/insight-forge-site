Daemons of the Shadow Realm Season 2 has officially been announced, giving fans confirmation that the supernatural anime will continue after its first season finale. The series is based on the manga by Hiromu Arakawa, the creator of Fullmetal Alchemist. The anime is produced by BONES FILM, with Masahiro Ando directing. What the Season 2 teaser shows

The first teaser is short but packed with returning characters. ORICON reports that it features Yuru, Asa, Left, Right, Gabby, Dera and Hana, alongside Ivan, who previously became involved in major battles with Yuru and his allies. The teaser also introduces Misasagi, a key figure connected to the Nishinomura faction targeting Yuru and Asa. citeturn1search3

One line from Yuru gives the teaser its tone: he tells viewers not to believe something until they see it with their own eyes. The rapid sequence of character shots does not reveal a complete plot, but it establishes that the second season will continue directly with the existing cast and conflicts.

No release date yet

One important detail is still missing: a release window has not been announced. Anime.com reports that the official announcement confirmed production but did not provide a premiere date, Season 2 staff list or complete cast information. More details are expected later. citeturn1search4

That means fans should treat any unofficial date circulating online as speculation until the production committee provides an official schedule.

Why the continuation matters

The first season established a world where Yuru and his twin sister Asa are connected to powerful supernatural Daemons, while different groups pursue their own interests around them. By the finale, the series had introduced a large group of characters and competing factions. Season 2 can therefore build on an established mythology instead of starting from scratch.

The manga has also developed a significant following. ORICON reports that the manga has surpassed six million copies in circulation, showing the scale of interest surrounding Arakawa's current series. citeturn1search3

What happens next?

The immediate next step is additional production information. Fans can expect future announcements to clarify the release window, returning staff and cast, and possibly a longer promotional video.

For now, the key news is confirmed: Daemons of the Shadow Realm Season 2 is in production. The teaser brings back the core characters and introduces signs of the next conflict, but the release date and full story details remain undisclosed.
