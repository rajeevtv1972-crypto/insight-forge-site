UNABOMBER Is Now Streaming on Netflix: Cast, Story and What the Film Covers

The new Netflix drama uses two timelines to explore the Unabomber case and the people caught up in it.

UNABOMBER is now streaming on Netflix after its September 25, 2026 release. Directed by Janus Metz, the drama is inspired by the true story of Ted Kaczynski and follows two connected timelines: one examining his early years and the other tracking the FBI investigation that ultimately located him. Netflix's official material lists Jacob Tremblay as the young Kaczynski, with Russell Crowe and Shailene Woodley among the principal cast.

What UNABOMBER is about

The film does not present the Unabomber case as a simple crime thriller. Instead, Netflix describes a story built around the path from Kaczynski's time at Harvard to the later investigation into the domestic terrorist attacks he carried out. The dual-timeline structure allows the film to move between personal history and the law-enforcement effort to identify and capture him.

That structure is important because the historical case covers decades. A conventional linear movie would have to compress a large amount of material into one sequence, while two timelines let the filmmakers connect early experiences with the investigation without presenting the story as a straight biography.

The cast and central performances

Jacob Tremblay plays the younger Ted Kaczynski, with Russell Crowe portraying Harvard professor Henry Murray and Shailene Woodley playing FBI agent Joanne Miller. Netflix's production material also lists Annabelle Wallis and other supporting performers.

The casting reflects the film's two sides. Tremblay anchors the earlier timeline, while Woodley's character provides a viewpoint closer to the investigators trying to understand who the Unabomber was and how the case might be solved. Crowe's character is tied to the Harvard portion of the story and the psychological context that the film explores.

Why the movie uses two timelines

Netflix's own discussion of the film explains that the writers were interested in the early years of Kaczynski's life as well as the eventual FBI pursuit. The resulting structure allows the audience to see the case from more than one period at once.

That also means the movie is less about suspense created by not knowing the final historical outcome. The Unabomber's identity is already established in public history. The drama instead comes from how the filmmakers reconstruct the human and investigative processes that surrounded the case.

Where the historical material begins and ends

UNABOMBER is inspired by real events, but it remains a dramatic production. Netflix identifies the historical basis while also presenting fictionalized characters and narrative choices for the adaptation.

That distinction matters when watching movies based on real cases. A film can use real names, places and events while still compressing timelines, combining people or shaping scenes for narrative clarity. Viewers interested in the documented history should use primary historical records or reputable reporting as a separate reference.

What to expect before pressing play

UNABOMBER is a serious crime drama inspired by a case involving real victims. It is therefore very different from a conventional fictional thriller. Netflix's description emphasizes the character history and investigation, so viewers looking for a lighter weekend movie may want to know the subject matter before starting.

The film is also more useful to approach as an interpretation of a historical case than as a complete documentary record. Its two timelines are designed to create narrative connections, while the real investigation involved many years of work and a much larger cast of people and evidence.
