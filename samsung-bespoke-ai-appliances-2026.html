Samsung's 2026 Bespoke AI Appliances

AI-powered home technology arrives in a broader Indian lineup

HomeAnimeMoviesTechSpaceGaming

Samsung India has launched its 2026 Bespoke AI appliance lineup, expanding its connected-home strategy across refrigerators, air conditioners and laundry products. The company says the range is designed around AI, SmartThings connectivity, security and personalization.

Family Hub Gets AI Vision and Gemini

The 32-inch Family Hub refrigerator includes contextual AI experiences and AI Vision features. Samsung says its implementation uses Google Gemini to support experiences such as video-to-recipe, while Family Care and Pet Care add connected monitoring features.

Smarter Cooling

The new Bespoke AI WindFree Pro air conditioner combines AI controls with PM1.0 purification and SmartThings connectivity. Samsung also introduced an Infinite 1-Way Cassette AC with WindFree technology, AI Energy Mode and draft-free cooling through thousands of micro-holes.

Security and Long-Term Updates

Samsung says enhanced Knox security is used across the ecosystem, while SmartThings software support can provide seven years of updates on supported appliances. Voice ID in Bixby is intended to personalize commands for individual household members.

India Pricing

Samsung's announced pricing includes the Infinite 1-Way Cassette AC starting at ₹1,60,000, Family Hub at ₹4,00,690 and Bespoke AI WindFree Pro AC at ₹69,990. Actual retail availability can vary by model and channel.
