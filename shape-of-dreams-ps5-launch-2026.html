Shape of Dreams Launches on PS5

Co-op roguelite gameplay, Travelers and the Starless Path update

Home Anime Movies Tech Space Gaming

Shape of Dreams has launched on PlayStation 5 on September 17, 2026, bringing its co-op roguelite experience to console players. The release arrives with The Starless Path, described by the developer as the game’s first-anniversary update and its largest update so far.

Developed by Lizard Smoothie, Shape of Dreams combines fast-paced combat with a dream world that changes each time players enter it. The game features nine different Travelers, each with distinct abilities and play styles. Players descend into a world known as the Rapids, where changing encounters create a different run from one attempt to the next.

That structure places the game firmly within the modern roguelite tradition. Instead of following exactly the same sequence every time, players learn through repeated attempts, experiment with different builds and gradually improve their understanding of enemies and abilities. The procedural approach can make each run feel different while still relying on a set of underlying systems that players can master.

The co-op aspect is another key feature. Shape of Dreams is designed around teamwork, allowing players to combine the abilities of different Travelers. The result is a combat system where builds can complement each other rather than simply increasing individual damage.

The Starless Path update is part of the PS5 launch and adds new content to the game. Launching a major update alongside the console version gives new players a larger starting experience while also providing existing players with a reason to return.

According to PlayStation’s official announcement, combat is designed to be aggressive and fast-paced. Players move through the Rapids while dealing with enemies and building their character around different combinations of skills. This creates a balance between immediate action and longer-term build decisions.

The dream setting also gives the game a distinctive visual and narrative framework. Rather than using a conventional fantasy kingdom or futuristic city, the developers describe the environment as a dream world that reshapes itself from one entry to another. That premise naturally supports changing levels and unpredictable encounters.

The September 17 launch also places Shape of Dreams inside a busy PS5 release schedule. The same month includes major games such as Marvel’s Wolverine and several Nintendo Switch 2 projects, so the game enters a crowded period for new releases.

For players who enjoy roguelites, one attraction is the learning curve. Every failed run can provide information about enemy patterns, abilities and build combinations. Co-op adds another layer because teams have to understand how their characters interact.

The PS5 version also benefits from console-specific features such as DualSense controller support. Console players receive a version built for a living-room setup rather than a simple port designed around keyboard and mouse controls.

The game is also notable because it arrives on PS5 with an established anniversary update rather than a bare launch version. That suggests the developer is treating the console release as part of a broader expansion of the game’s audience.

Ultimately, Shape of Dreams is built around a straightforward loop: choose a Traveler, enter the shifting dream world, fight through enemies, experiment with builds and return with new knowledge. The co-op design gives friends another reason to replay the same core systems in different combinations.

With its September 17 PS5 launch and The Starless Path update, Shape of Dreams is now positioned as a fresh option for players looking for action, experimentation and repeatable co-op runs.
