BATTLEGROUNDS MOBILE INDIA is getting a new grassroots esports competition with BGMI Gully Wars, announced in September 2026 by KRAFTON India. The open-for-all tournament carries a reported ₹10 lakh prize pool and is designed to give players a path from in-game competition toward organized esports.

The tournament is particularly relevant for Indian mobile gamers because registration is open beyond established professional organizations. According to current reporting, registrations remain open until October 6, followed by in-game qualifiers and elimination stages.

Registration deadline

Players interested in participating need to register before October 6, 2026. The competition is structured to allow a large pool of players to enter before the field is reduced through competitive stages.

Exact eligibility requirements, team rules and registration instructions should be checked through KRAFTON India's official esports channels because tournament rules can be updated before qualifiers begin.

How the tournament progresses

The current schedule places the In-Game Qualifier from October 14 to October 20. After that stage, teams progress through elimination rounds that eventually reduce the field to the final 16 teams.

The Grand Finals will then decide the final standings and prize distribution. The structure gives players a clear competitive ladder instead of requiring them to enter directly into a small professional bracket.

Why grassroots esports matters

BGMI already has a large competitive ecosystem in India, and KRAFTON's broader 2026 esports roadmap includes established properties such as BGIS, BMPS, BMSD and BMIC alongside open tournaments.

Open competitions such as Gully Wars can serve a different purpose from professional leagues. They provide less-established players with an opportunity to compete publicly and potentially attract attention from teams, creators and the wider esports community.

What players should prepare

Players planning to enter should focus on team coordination, consistent practice and familiarity with the current BGMI competitive environment. Tournament performance can depend on communication and decision-making as much as individual aim.

Teams should also make sure every member understands the tournament rules, registration requirements and match schedule before qualifiers begin. Missing a deadline or failing an eligibility requirement can prevent a team from participating even after extensive preparation.

The ₹10 lakh prize pool

The reported ₹10 lakh prize pool gives the event a meaningful competitive incentive while keeping the focus on grassroots participation. The exact distribution across final positions should be confirmed from KRAFTON's official tournament information.

For players, the value of the event is not only the prize money. A strong performance can also provide competitive experience in a structured tournament setting.

Key dates

October 6: current registration deadline.

October 14–20: In-Game Qualifier window.

After qualifiers: elimination rounds narrow the field to 16 teams before the Grand Finals.

What happens next

BGMI Gully Wars adds another open competitive pathway to India's growing mobile esports scene. With registration already underway and qualifiers scheduled for October, players have a clear window to organize teams and prepare.

The most important next step is checking KRAFTON India's official tournament information for the latest rules and registration status before the October deadline.
