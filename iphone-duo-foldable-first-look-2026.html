iPhone Duo: Apple's First Foldable iPhone Explained

Displays, hinge design, cameras, iOS 27 and launch timing

Apple has officially entered the foldable-phone market with iPhone Duo, its first foldable iPhone. Announced on September 9, 2026, the device opens into a 7.6-inch inner display and closes around a 5.4-inch outer display. Two displays built around one design

The main attraction is the dual-screen approach. When unfolded, the 7.6-inch display creates a larger canvas for watching video, playing games and using multiple apps. When closed, the outer 5.4-inch display keeps the phone compact enough for everyday use. Apple says both displays share the same aspect ratio so content can scale proportionally as the phone changes position. citeturn854018search1

Reimagined iOS 27

iPhone Duo is not simply an iPhone with a larger folding screen. Apple has redesigned parts of the iOS 27 experience around different orientations and poses. The company highlights side-by-side apps, hands-free FaceTime and flexible viewing angles as examples of what the foldable format enables. That software layer is important because a foldable's usefulness depends as much on how apps respond to the extra screen as on the hinge itself. citeturn854018search3turn854018search4

A20 Pro and thermal engineering

Under the foldable design is Apple's A20 Pro chip, paired with an advanced thermal system and a custom-designed vapor chamber. Apple also uses a dual-battery architecture. The goal is to combine the performance expected from a Pro-class iPhone with the extra demands of a larger foldable display. citeturn854018search1

Cameras designed for the folding form

The iPhone Duo uses a 48MP Dual Fusion camera system with a 48MP Fusion Ultra Wide and 48MP Fusion Main camera, plus a 12MP Center Stage front camera. Apple also highlights foldable-specific features such as Smart Take for hands-free photography and Duo Preview, which uses the outer display for live previews. These features are designed to make the hinge an active part of the camera experience rather than just a way to change screen size. citeturn854018search3

Why iPhone Duo matters

iPhone Duo represents a major change in Apple's phone hardware strategy because it combines the familiar iPhone platform with a foldable form factor. The larger inner display, redesigned iOS experience and camera features create a different use case from the conventional iPhone 18 Pro family.
