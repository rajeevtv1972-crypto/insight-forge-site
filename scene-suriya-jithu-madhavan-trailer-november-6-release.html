The makers released the trailer for Jithu Madhavan’s Tamil film Scene on October 31, showing Suriya as police officer Inbaraj and teasing a crime story with action, mystery and comedy.

The trailer for Scene, the upcoming Tamil film starring Suriya and directed by Jithu Madhavan, was released on October 31, 2026. The film is scheduled to reach cinemas on November 6, giving the makers less than a week between the full trailer reveal and its planned theatrical opening.

The trailer introduces Suriya as Inbaraj, a police officer who appears to have stepped away from the force before being pulled back into an unfinished murder investigation. Lal plays a senior officer who asks him to return to the case. The footage suggests that the investigation will bring Inbaraj into conflict with Eeswaran, played by Naslen.

A mix of police procedural, action and comedy

The promotional material positions Scene as more than a straight crime drama. Its trailer moves between police-station exchanges, action scenes and comic moments, with Nazriya Nazim also presented in a role that appears to have more to it than the first impression. The central hook is the unresolved case and the relationship between Inbaraj and the adversary he is asked to confront.

Jithu Madhavan’s earlier work has made audiences curious about how he will balance humour and tension in a film led by a major Tamil star. The trailer gives a clearer look at the characters and the film’s tone, but it does not reveal enough to settle the mystery at the centre of the plot.

Release and cast details

The film stars Suriya, Nazriya Nazim, Naslen and Lal, with additional supporting cast members. The makers are targeting a worldwide theatrical release on November 6. Showtimes and language availability should be checked with local cinemas as bookings open.

With the trailer now out, the next meaningful measure will be audience response to the film itself. The final verdict will depend on whether the story delivers a satisfying investigation alongside the action and comedy promised by the promotional campaign.
