October 2026 Horror Streaming Guide: Paramount+ Picks for Halloween

The best horror marathon is built around tone and audience—not simply the biggest collection of scary titles.

Published October 16, 2026 · Insight Forge
HomeAnimeMoviesTechnologySpaceGamingLatestSearch

Paramount+’s official October movie list contains a wide mix of horror and Halloween-adjacent titles, including Doctor Sleep, Drag Me to Hell, It Follows, Parasite and Sweeney Todd: The Demon Barber of Fleet Street. It also lists family-friendly selections such as Hotel Transylvania and its sequel. Because catalogues and plan eligibility can vary, the service’s own page is the place to confirm availability before organising a marathon.

Start with the mood, not the title count

Psychological horror, supernatural stories, satire and creature features create very different nights. Doctor Sleep is a sequel connected to a familiar horror universe, while It Follows builds unease around a persistent threat. Drag Me to Hell leans into a heightened supernatural premise. Choosing a consistent tone can make a double feature feel deliberate instead of like a random shuffle.

Keep audience and intensity in mind

A service catalogue can place family animation and adult horror on the same broad October list. Hotel Transylvania is a very different choice from a mature horror title, so check age ratings and content guidance if the viewing group includes younger people. The person choosing the film should consider comfort levels, not assume that every title labelled as a Halloween pick suits every household.

A few ways to arrange the night

For a supernatural double feature, pair two films with very different visual styles. For a lighter evening, choose a family animation and a familiar comedy. For a longer marathon, alternate the intensity so that every film does not rely on the same jump-scare rhythm. A short break between titles also gives everyone a chance to reset rather than streaming continuously by default.

Check subscription details before promising a title

Paramount+ notes that some films require the Premium plan and that release dates for library titles can change. A film listed in an editorial guide is not a guarantee that it is available in every country or under every subscription. Open the title page in the account you plan to use and confirm the current details before you send a watch-party invitation.

Make the list work for your group

Pick three options—scary, lighter and backup—and agree on the tone before starting. If a film is too intense, switch to the backup rather than treating the marathon as a challenge. The most useful streaming list does more than name movies: it helps viewers choose something appropriate, verify availability and spend less time browsing.
