Urban Myth Dissolution Center Arrives on Switch 2 With Two Notable Extras

The Nintendo Switch 2 edition launched on October 28 with a gallery feature and Joy-Con 2 mouse controls highlighted in the release information.

31 October 2026 · Insight Forge
HomeAnimeMoviesTechSpaceGamingAutomobileSportsLatest

Urban Myth Dissolution Center has arrived in a Nintendo Switch 2 edition, with the release dated October 28, 2026. The updated version gives the mystery adventure a new platform home and highlights two additions: a gallery feature and support for Joy-Con 2 mouse controls.

The gallery is a welcome extra for a narrative-led game because it gives players a place to revisit visual material after progressing through the story. Mouse-style input is also a natural fit for an investigation interface, where players inspect details and navigate evidence rather than relying only on fast action controls.

What is different in the Switch 2 edition?

The two headline features are presentation and input. A gallery gives the game’s art and visual details a home beyond the initial playthrough, while the Joy-Con 2 mouse function offers another way to interact with menus and investigative elements. These are quality-of-life and presentation additions; they should not be mistaken for an entirely new story campaign.

Players should check the store listing in their region for the exact edition details, upgrade options and pricing. Availability and purchase terms can differ between territories, especially where an original Switch release and a separate Switch 2 edition are listed together.

Who should take a look?

The new edition is most relevant to players who own a Switch 2 and have not yet played the mystery, or fans who want to revisit it with the added gallery feature. Existing owners should compare the edition and upgrade information carefully before buying again.

The official Nintendo listing is the best place to confirm compatibility and the product being purchased. The useful headline is that the Switch 2 version is available, with platform-specific touches rather than a completely different game.
