India’s November 2026 Movie Calendar: What to Watch in Theatres

November’s Indian cinema calendar is filling up, with Ramayana: Part One among the major titles listed for the opening week. Release dates and regional schedules can still change.

31 October 2026 · Insight Forge

November is shaping up to be a busy month for Indian cinema, with films arriving across languages and a mix of large-scale theatrical releases and smaller regional titles. One of the most prominent dates in current release roundups is November 5, when Ramayana: Part One is listed for its reported worldwide opening.

Release calendars are useful for planning, but they are not the same as a final cinema schedule. Distributors may shift dates, regional versions may open on different days, and local showtimes normally appear closer to release. Treat aggregator listings as a watchlist and verify final details with the distributor or ticketing platform.

How to read the November calendar

Start with the first week, where release roundups cluster titles around the November 5–8 window. Then check the middle and final weeks for additions. A title appearing on a calendar means it has been reported or scheduled by a listing service; it does not guarantee that every language version or city will receive a day-one show.

For multilingual releases, search by both the original title and the language version you want to watch. This avoids confusion when a film has different promotional names, dubbed versions or region-specific release dates.

A simple checklist before you book

  • Confirm the date from the film’s official account, distributor or studio.
  • Check the language, subtitles, format and censor certificate shown by the cinema.
  • Recheck showtimes on the day you plan to go; listings can change as screens are allocated.

The November slate will become more reliable as distributors publish final campaign details. Use release calendars to discover titles, not as a guarantee that a particular show is available in your city.
