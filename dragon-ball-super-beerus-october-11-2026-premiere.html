Dragon Ball Super: Beerus Premieres October 11, 2026: Trailer, Themes and NYCC Panel

Latest news and confirmed details

HomeAnimeMoviesTechSpaceGaming

Dragon Ball Super: Beerus is set to return in October 2026, with the new anime scheduled to premiere in Japan on October 11, 2026. The Dragon Ball Official Site has confirmed a new “Super Surge” visual and trailer, along with the opening and ending theme artists.

The new series is built around an enhanced presentation of the Dragon Ball Super material and arrives with a major promotional event at New York Comic Con 2026. The first episode will receive a world-premiere advance screening during the convention's Dragon Ball Super: Beerus SPECIAL PANEL.

October 11 premiere

The anime will air on Fuji TV at 11:15 p.m. in Japan, taking over the time slot currently occupied by ONE PIECE. The official announcement also says the fastest streaming release will follow shortly after the television broadcast on Prime Video and U-NEXT, with other streaming services scheduled to follow on Thursdays.

For international viewers, exact availability can vary by territory and platform, so local streaming schedules should be checked closer to launch.

A new trailer and “Super Surge” visual

The latest promotional material introduces the Super Surge visual and a new trailer featuring footage from the upcoming anime. The campaign is clearly positioning the series as a major Dragon Ball event for the fall anime season.

The project credits Akira Toriyama for the original work, story and character design. Matsuto Seya is credited with composition and direction arrangement, while Tadayoshi Yamamuro serves as animation character designer and general animation director.

Opening and ending themes

The opening theme, “ONOGAMI,” will be performed by MEGATERA・ZERO. The ending theme, “Kan Peki No Peki,” will be performed by VK Blanka.

Theme-song announcements are an important part of the final promotional build-up because they give fans another preview of the tone and presentation of the new series before the first episode arrives.

New York Comic Con gets the first episode

Dragon Ball Super: Beerus will also appear at New York Comic Con 2026, which runs October 8–11. The special panel is scheduled for October 8 at the Javits Center and will include a world-premiere advance screening of the first episode.

English dub voice actors Jason Douglas, who voices Beerus, and Ian Sinclair, who voices Whis, are listed as panel guests, with Monica Rial hosting. Attendees will also receive Dragon Ball-themed giveaways.

What fans should expect

The combination of a new trailer, confirmed theme songs, a fixed October 11 premiere and an NYCC screening means the marketing campaign is already entering its final stage. More information about regional streaming and additional promotional material can arrive before launch.

For now, the key date is October 11, 2026 in Japan. Dragon Ball Super: Beerus is one of the major returning anime titles of the fall, and the New York Comic Con screening gives international fans an early look at the first episode.
