Variable aperture changes the camera experience

The standout camera change is the 48MP Fusion Main camera's variable aperture. Instead of relying on a single fixed opening, the lens can adjust how much light reaches the sensor. That gives photographers more control over low-light shots, depth of field and the overall look of an image. A20 Pro and improved sustained performance

The iPhone 18 Pro uses Apple's new A20 Pro chip and pairs it with a redesigned vapor chamber. The combination is intended to maintain high performance for longer during demanding activities such as gaming, video processing and AI workloads.
