The official site currently lists Frozen Trail for Steam, Epic Games Store, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and GeForce NOW. That makes the update a cross-platform release rather than a PC-only event.

What is Frozen Trail?

Frozen Trail is the name of the October update, and the first-look announcement was published on September 23. The developers are teasing a colder environment and new content without revealing every detail in advance.

The official site also displays changing map conditions, showing that ARC Raiders' world can rotate through different situations and activity windows. That system gives the extraction loop another layer because players are not simply entering a static map every time.

Why map conditions matter

Extraction shooters depend heavily on information. Knowing where to go, what resources may appear and what threats are active can determine whether a run succeeds. Dynamic conditions can change those calculations and encourage players to adapt rather than follow one fixed route.

Frozen Trail appears to build on that concept by adding a distinct seasonal identity. The colder theme can affect the way players approach exploration, even before the developers reveal every gameplay detail.

PC and console players get the update

The official ARC Raiders website lists the October 8 update across PC storefronts, current-generation consoles and GeForce NOW. Players therefore have several ways to access the new content. citeturn1search3

Another important development: PvE discussion

PC Gamer reported on September 24 that ARC Raiders is testing a PvE-only mode, with the studio saying it cannot ignore what player data is telling it. This is separate from the Frozen Trail announcement, but it shows that the developers are actively examining how different players want to experience the game. citeturn1search7
