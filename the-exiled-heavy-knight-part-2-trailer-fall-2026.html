The Exiled Heavy Knight Part 2 Reveals New Trailer, Visual, Cast and Themes

Latest news and confirmed details · September 24, 2026

HomeAnimeMoviesTechSpaceGaming

The Exiled Heavy Knight Knows How to Game the System is moving into its next major phase, with Part 2 revealing a new key visual, main trailer, additional cast members, and two new theme songs. The new material was announced on September 23, 2026, ahead of the series continuing on Crunchyroll in Fall 2026.

The update gives viewers a clearer idea of what comes next for Elymas, the reincarnated Heavy Knight who understands the game-like world around him better than the people who underestimate his class. The new promotional material also expands the supporting cast, giving the second part of the anime more characters to build around as the story moves into new quests and battles.

Part 2 gets a new trailer and visual

The new main trailer is designed as a transition point between the first part of the story and the adventures that follow. Alongside new footage, the production has released a fresh key visual for Part 2. The combination signals that the anime is treating the continuation as a significant new chapter rather than simply a routine batch of episodes.

The series is being animated by GoHands, with the staff continuing to handle the distinctive action-heavy presentation. The anime is scheduled to continue streaming on Crunchyroll during Fall 2026, following the earlier confirmation that the television run spans two consecutive cours.

Four new characters join the cast

Part 2 introduces four additional characters whose abilities and relationships should broaden Elymas' journey. Kosuke Tanabe voices Kelt, a B-rank Hunter who joins Elymas on a raid quest. Tsugumi Sakuragi voices Maebel, a C-rank Priest whose role adds another type of support to the expanding group.

Misaki Kuno joins as Hilde, a B-rank Magic Swordsman who describes herself as Carlos' foremost disciple. Kengo Kawanishi voices Carlos, an A-rank Magic Swordsman known as the Black Flame Blade. The four characters create a wider range of combat and party dynamics for the continuation.

New opening and ending songs

The Part 2 opening theme is Rewrite the Answer performed by Tokoyami Towa, while PompadollS performs the ending theme Contemporary Dance. New theme songs are more than a marketing detail for a long-running seasonal anime: they also help establish the identity of a new story phase and give returning viewers a fresh audio introduction to the characters.

Why the second part matters to the story

The premise remains one of the anime's central hooks. Elma is reborn in a world that matches a game he once played, so information that looks ordinary to other characters becomes an advantage for him. The Heavy Knight class is treated as weak or unfashionable by many people in the setting, but Elma knows the underlying mechanics and uses that knowledge to unlock its potential.

That setup allows the series to mix reincarnation, fantasy adventure and game-system logic. Part 2 can build on that idea by introducing stronger opponents, new locations and characters who have their own expertise rather than relying entirely on Elma's past-life knowledge.

What to watch before Part 2

For viewers catching up, the first part is the essential starting point because it establishes Elma's unusual advantage and explains why his Heavy Knight abilities become central to the story. Watching the earlier episodes also makes the arrival of Kelt, Maebel, Hilde and Carlos easier to follow.

With a new visual, main trailer, four additional cast members and new theme songs all arriving together, the Fall 2026 continuation has a clear promotional push behind it. Fans now have a better look at the direction of the next chapter while the anime prepares to continue its two-cour story.
