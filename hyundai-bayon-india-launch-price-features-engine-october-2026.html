Hyundai BAYON Launches in India at ₹9.99 Lakh: Engine, Tech and Safety

The new SUV arrives with a familiar 1.5-litre petrol powertrain but places a strong emphasis on connected technology, driver assistance and a modern cabin.

The new SUV arrives with a familiar 1.5-litre petrol powertrain but places a strong emphasis on connected technology, driver assistance and a modern cabin.

Price and powertrain

Hyundai Motor India announced the BAYON on October 26, 2026, at a starting price of ₹9,99,900 ex-showroom. The India-spec model uses a four-cylinder 1.5-litre MPi petrol engine rated at 84.4 kW, or 115 PS, and 143.8 Nm of torque. Buyers can choose a six-speed manual or Hyundai's Intelligent Variable Transmission (iVT).

A technology-led cabin

The company highlights a Connected Car Navigation Cockpit with two 12.3-inch curved displays, Hyundai Bluelink connected features and over-the-air updates for supported controllers. Hyundai also says the BAYON introduces its first generative-AI voice assistant in India, combining technology from Perplexity and SoundHound AI. As with any connected feature, the final experience can depend on software, network availability, subscriptions and variant equipment.

Driver assistance and safety claims

Hyundai lists SmartSense Level 2 Pro ADAS with 23 driver-assistance features. The company says the vehicle is built on its Global K Enhanced Platform and engineered around Indian safety standards. That wording should not be confused with an independent crash-test rating: buyers should check the exact variant equipment and look for verified test results where available.

Who might consider it

The manual version may appeal to drivers who want a conventional gearbox, while the iVT is aimed at those who prefer a smoother automatic-style experience in traffic. The 1.5-litre engine is familiar within Hyundai's Indian line-up, but buyers should test the vehicle on their regular routes and compare rear-seat space, boot capacity, visibility and real-world fuel consumption with alternatives in the same price band.

What to verify before booking

Ask the dealer for the complete variant-wise price list, insurance and registration charges, delivery estimate, warranty details and the list of ADAS features included in the chosen trim. Check whether connected services require a trial or paid renewal. At ₹9.99 lakh ex-showroom for the entry version, the final on-road amount will be higher and will vary by city and variant.

Insight Forge takeaway: Hyundai has launched the BAYON in India from ₹9.99 lakh ex-showroom, with a 115 PS petrol engine, two transmission choices and a feature-rich cockpit.
