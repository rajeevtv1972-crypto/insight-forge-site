Marvel's Wolverine

Insomniac's PS5 action-adventure is now available

HomeAnimeMoviesTechSpaceGaming

Marvel's Wolverine launched worldwide on September 15, 2026, exclusively for PlayStation 5. Sony Interactive Entertainment says the game is developed by Insomniac Games in collaboration with Marvel Games and centers on Logan as he searches for answers about his past.

What Kind of Game Is It?

PlayStation describes it as a single-player, narrative-driven action-adventure. Unlike Insomniac's Spider-Man games, it is not an open-world title. The focus is a more tightly directed adventure built around Wolverine's mobility, combat and story.

Combat and Abilities

Wolverine's adamantium claws form the core of the combat system. Players can use techniques and adaptations to approach encounters through aggression, ambushes and momentum-based attacks. DualSense haptics and adaptive triggers are also used to make combat feel more physical.

Story and Characters

The official material highlights Logan's search for answers, while the game's developer interviews discuss the design of Wolverine, his allies, enemies and locations. The story combines a personal journey with large action set pieces.

Release Status

Marvel's Wolverine is available now on PlayStation 5. Sony's official release announcement confirms the worldwide launch date and platform.
