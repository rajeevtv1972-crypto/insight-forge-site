Black Clover Season 2 Returns October 3, 2026: What to Know Before Asta's Comeback

Episode 171 starts the next chapter of the Spade Kingdom conflict

HomeAnimeMoviesTechSpaceGamingLatestFAQ
Anime · Black Clover · Published 29 Sep 2026

Black Clover is finally ready to return. Season 2 is scheduled to premiere on October 3, 2026, bringing the anime back to weekly television after the long gap that followed the original series. The first new episode is numbered Episode 171 overall and is titled “War Begins,” a fitting signal that the story is jumping directly back into the escalating Spade Kingdom conflict.

A return that has been years in the making

The first Black Clover TV run concluded with Episode 170 in March 2021. That left fans with a substantial unfinished stretch of manga material and years of waiting for a continuation. Season 2 turns that waiting period into a major comeback moment for one of the modern shonen series built around long-form rivalries, battles and character progression.

The October 3 premiere is also notable because it is not being positioned as a brand-new starting point. The official preview material points directly toward the consequences of the previous arc, meaning returning viewers can expect the story to move back into the central conflict rather than spend the opening episode rebuilding the world from scratch.

At a glance: Season 2 premieres October 3, 2026. Episode 171 is titled “War Begins,” Crunchyroll is among the streaming destinations, and Studio Pierrot remains in charge of animation production.

Where the story picks up

The next chapter continues the battle around the Clover Kingdom, the Spade Kingdom and the dangerous forces connected to the Dark Triad. Asta’s development remains one of the biggest reasons the return matters. His relationship with Liebe and the pursuit of greater control over his abilities are central to the story’s next stage.

Yuno is also heading into a much more personal stretch of the narrative. After earlier setbacks involving Zenon, his role in the coming conflict becomes increasingly tied to his growth as a Magic Knight and his determination to protect the people around him. The season therefore has room to push both sides of the Asta-Yuno rivalry while the larger war expands.

Returning staff and production

Season 2 brings back director Ayataka Tanemura, with Keiichiro Ochi handling series composition and Itsuko Takeda returning for character design. Studio Pierrot continues as the animation studio, keeping the sequel connected to the visual identity established in the first series.

Music is being handled by Minako Seki. The opening theme is “Kienai Riyuu” by WANIMA, adding a new musical identity to the comeback while the series returns to its battle-heavy storyline.

Episode 171 sets the tone

The title “War Begins” is more than a simple label. It tells viewers that the sequel is entering a part of Black Clover where the consequences of earlier battles begin to compound. Training, rivalries and power-ups are important, but they matter because the characters are preparing for conflicts that affect entire kingdoms.

That structure is one reason Black Clover has been able to sustain its appeal. Individual fights have clear emotional stakes, but they also serve a much wider story involving magic, politics inside the kingdoms and the growing influence of demonic powers. Season 2 starts with that larger framework already established.

Where to watch

Crunchyroll has included Black Clover Season 2 in its Fall 2026 lineup, with the English-dub availability listed for October 3. International availability can vary by territory, so viewers should check the service available in their region before the premiere.

For Indian viewers, that timing makes the October 3 return particularly easy to plan around because Crunchyroll's Fall 2026 lineup covers the Indian Subcontinent. Exact local release times can depend on the streaming service and regional scheduling.

Why this comeback matters

Long anime gaps can make a return difficult because audiences have to remember characters, powers and unfinished plot threads. Black Clover has an advantage: the manga story provides a clear route forward, and the sequel starts at a point where the stakes were already high.

The bigger question is not whether there is enough story left to adapt. There is. The interesting question is how the anime translates the next stretch of material into weekly episodes, especially the battles where animation, pacing and choreography can have a huge impact on the viewing experience.

What fans should watch for

Before October 3, it is worth refreshing the major events from the end of the first anime run: Asta’s relationship with Liebe, Yuno’s connection to the Spade Kingdom conflict, the Dark Triad, the status of the Black Bulls and the consequences of the previous battles. Those pieces form the bridge between the original run and Episode 171.

Black Clover Season 2 is therefore more than another seasonal premiere. It is the continuation of a story that has been waiting for its next animated chapter for years. With a confirmed October 3 debut, returning production staff and a story that immediately moves toward war, the stage is set for Asta and the Black Bulls to step back into the spotlight.
