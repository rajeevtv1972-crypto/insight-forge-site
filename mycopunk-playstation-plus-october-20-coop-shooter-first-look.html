Mycopunk Joins PlayStation Plus on October 20: A Co-op First Look

A four-player squad, upgrade experimentation and repeated missions make Mycopunk a title to consider with a ready-made co-op group.

Published October 16, 2026 · Insight Forge
HomeAnimeMoviesTechnologySpaceGamingLatestSearch

Sony’s October 14 PlayStation Blog post says Mycopunk will arrive day one in the PlayStation Plus Game Catalog on October 20 worldwide. The company describes it as a mission-based looter shooter with customisable upgrades, four-player co-op and a squad of robot rejects sent to investigate a planet-wide fungal threat. Those details make it a natural choice for groups who prefer short missions and gear experimentation over a long single-player campaign.

What the game’s pitch suggests

The official description centres on a team called the New Atlas Hazard Crew, tasked with confronting fungal aliens and investigating the cause of a planetary disaster. The looter-shooter structure implies an emphasis on repeated missions and equipment improvement. Players should confirm the game’s current store details for progression systems, supported modes and any additional requirements rather than treating a short catalogue summary as a full feature list.

Why co-op roles can shape the experience

A four-player game is often more enjoyable when everyone has a clear role: one person can lead navigation, another can monitor objectives, and the group can coordinate upgrades. That does not mean players need a strict competitive build. Early sessions are a good time to try different equipment and discover whether the game rewards specialisation or a flexible squad.

Make use of the catalog window

The October Game Catalog lineup is scheduled to become available on October 20 unless otherwise noted. If you plan to play day one, check that your account’s subscription tier includes the title and pre-load or queue updates when possible. Storefront availability and subscription catalogues can differ by region, so verify the local page before assembling a group.

Questions to answer after launch

Once the game is available, players can evaluate mission variety, enemy patterns, how meaningful the upgrade choices feel and whether co-op matchmaking works well for their group. Those practical factors matter more over several weeks than a stylish premise. Early patches may also adjust balance, so keep an eye on official updates before assuming a first-day impression is final.

The takeaway

Mycopunk looks aimed at friends who like experimenting with loadouts and tackling missions together. Its October 20 catalog release gives eligible subscribers a low-friction way to try it. Confirm the final availability in your region, invite the group, and judge the game by how well its missions and progression hold up after the first few sessions.
