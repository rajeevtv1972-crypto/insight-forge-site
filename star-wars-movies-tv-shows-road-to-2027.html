Star Wars on the Road to 2027: Movies and Series to Watch

A franchise preparing for its 50th anniversary

A look at Star Wars projects discussed for 2027 and beyond, separating announced plans from projects still in development.

A major anniversary approaches

Star Wars reaches its 50th anniversary in 2027, giving Lucasfilm and Disney a natural moment to bring the franchise back into focus. The publicly discussed slate includes Star Wars: Starfighter, scheduled for May 2027, and a second season of Ahsoka reported for January 2027.

A new story can be a fresh start

Starfighter is positioned as a new story rather than a direct retelling of the Skywalker saga. That may appeal to viewers who want the atmosphere of Star Wars without revisiting every film and series first. New characters and a distinct period can help a long-running universe feel open again.

Development is not a release guarantee

Projects in development can be delayed, reworked or paused as scripts, production schedules and business priorities change. Treat dates for projects without active production confirmation as provisional, and distinguish official announcements from rumor accounts.

How series fit together

Streaming shows can explore corners of the galaxy that a two-hour film has little time to visit. But crossovers can overwhelm viewers. Before starting a new season, check whether it continues a specific character’s story or is designed to stand alone.

What fans should watch for

The most useful signals are official release-date announcements, production updates and confirmed casting—not every rumor on social media. The next wave will need a balance of accessible standalone stories and meaningful continuity.
