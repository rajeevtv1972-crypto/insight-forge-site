Frequently Asked Questions

What is Insight Forge?

Insight Forge covers technology, anime, movies, space, science and gaming through news stories, explainers, guides and analysis.

How do I ask a question?

Scroll to the Community Discussion section and publish a top-level comment containing your question or topic. Other readers can use Reply to respond to you.

Can readers reply to one another?

Yes. Disqus supports replies inside the discussion thread, so a reader can answer another reader directly.

Can I post without a Disqus account?

Yes, when Guest Commenting is enabled for the Insight Forge Disqus forum. Guest commenters can post without creating a Disqus profile. Guest comments are subject to Disqus moderation rules.

Where can I discuss a specific article?

Every article can have its own Disqus discussion. The FAQ page is the general community space for questions that are not tied to one particular article.

How do I get notified about replies?

Registered Disqus users can receive reply notifications, and readers can also subscribe to discussions where supported by Disqus.
