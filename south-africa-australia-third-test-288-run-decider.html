South Africa Set Australia 288 in a Newlands Test Decider

A 288-run target has set up the final day of the tied Test series at Newlands. This is a pre-play snapshot; the live match situation may change during the day.

The deciding Test between South Africa and Australia has reached a tense final-day setup at Newlands. South Africa’s second innings ended at 387, setting Australia a target of 288 runs to win. The series is level at 1–1, so the result will decide the contest.

Ryan Rickelton made 101 and Wiaan Mulder scored 115 in South Africa’s second innings, giving the hosts the platform to build a challenging target. Their contributions came after the match had swung between the sides, leaving Australia with a chase that is possible but far from straightforward on the final day.

Why the target matters

A fourth-innings chase of 288 is a test of both batting discipline and time management. Australia must balance the need to score with the risk of losing wickets, while South Africa can use the pitch, scoreboard pressure and attacking fields to create chances. The early sessions are likely to shape whether the chase becomes realistic or the home side gains control.

The series stakes add another layer. With the teams level, a win would settle the series and carry significance for the teams’ position at the top of the Test rankings. That makes every session more important than a routine final-day chase.
