September 2026 Movie Watchlist

New Netflix films, documentaries and returning favorites to watch

September 2026 brings a broad mix of movies and film-related releases to Netflix, ranging from new originals and documentaries to returning favorites and feature-length adaptations. For viewers who prefer streaming over theatrical releases, the month offers a convenient way to build a weekend watchlist without waiting for titles to arrive on digital rental stores.

Netflix’s September schedule includes a mixture of genres. Among the highlighted releases are Call My Agent! The Movie, Why Did I Get Married Again?, Crew Girl and other new films arriving throughout the month. The platform is also adding documentaries and older classics, making the schedule wider than a traditional theatrical slate.

Call My Agent! The Movie arrives as a feature continuation of the popular French series. Netflix describes the story as taking place several years after the agency closed, with Andréa returning to the world of film production after losing a lead actor shortly before filming begins. The movie therefore uses the original show’s workplace and entertainment-industry setting as the foundation for a new feature-length story.

Another September release is Why Did I Get Married Again?, which follows the characters into a new family gathering filled with relationship drama. The film gives viewers a sequel-style continuation rather than a completely unrelated story, making it a natural choice for audiences already familiar with the earlier movies.

Netflix is also releasing Crew Girl, a coming-of-age drama centered around a teenage rower who moves to a new town after a family scandal. She joins an elite prep school crew team and enters a social environment that creates new friendships, rivalries and personal complications.

September also contains documentary releases. Netflix’s schedule includes Untold projects covering sports and real-world personalities, along with films such as Turning Point: Generation 9/11. This mixture means subscribers can shift between fictional entertainment and documentary storytelling within the same month.

The service is also using September to bring back older movies. Netflix’s monthly schedule includes titles such as About Time, 17 Again and the Alien films, offering viewers a mixture of romance, comedy and science fiction. These catalog additions can be especially useful for audiences looking for familiar films between new releases.

One advantage of a streaming-first movie calendar is flexibility. The traditional theatrical model concentrates attention on a smaller number of big releases, while streaming platforms can add several titles every week. That creates more choices, but it also makes discovery more difficult. A clear watchlist can help viewers decide which movies fit their preferred genre rather than simply browsing a large catalog.

September 2026 also shows how streaming services are combining original films with established franchises. New releases attract viewers who want something unfamiliar, while recognizable properties can bring audiences back to the service.

The monthly lineup may vary by country, and Netflix notes that availability is subject to regional differences. Indian viewers should therefore check their local Netflix catalog before planning around a particular title.

For movie fans, September is less about one single blockbuster and more about variety. Call My Agent! The Movie offers a returning ensemble in a feature format, Why Did I Get Married Again? continues a comedy-drama franchise, Crew Girl targets a younger coming-of-age audience, and catalog additions provide well-known films for repeat viewing.

With new titles arriving throughout the month, Netflix’s September schedule gives viewers plenty of options across drama, comedy, documentaries, horror and science fiction.
