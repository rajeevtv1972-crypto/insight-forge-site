The story setup

The exact plot details have been kept relatively guarded, but the film's genre combination points toward a mystery built around folklore and an unusual force connected to the forest. That combination gives the movie room to mix investigation, supernatural mythology and character drama.

For audiences who enjoy Indian horror that draws from local legends, the setting is arguably the most interesting part of the premise. A forest can function as more than a location: it can become the source of the mystery, a visual symbol and a threat that the characters cannot easily understand.

Why the folk-horror angle matters

Folk horror works best when supernatural ideas feel connected to a specific place and culture. Instead of using a generic haunted-house setup, the genre can build fear around traditions, legends and environments that have their own history.

The Vvaan's forest-centered premise gives it an opportunity to do exactly that. The challenge will be balancing mythology with a coherent mystery so that the supernatural elements feel like part of the story rather than simply visual decoration.
