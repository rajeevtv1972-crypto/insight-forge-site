Apex Legends x Street Fighter 6 Event Launches September 22: New Modes, Cosmetics and Olympus

Latest news and confirmed details

Apex Legends is crossing over with Street Fighter 6 as the new event launches on September 22, 2026. Respawn and EA announced the collaboration ahead of its release, promising Street Fighter-inspired cosmetics and a combat-focused experience built around a remixed version of Olympus.

The event brings one of fighting games' most recognizable brands into the Apex universe. Players can use cosmetics based on Street Fighter characters while dropping into a map environment redesigned around the collaboration's theme.

What is changing?

The biggest attraction is the crossover itself. EA describes the event as two competitive worlds meeting in one Arena, with Hadokens, Shoryukens and Street Fighter-themed cosmetics appearing in the Outlands. The remixed Olympus is designed to give the event a different visual and gameplay atmosphere from ordinary matches.

The collaboration is part of Apex's broader collection of limited-time events. These events typically give players a short window to collect themed items and experience a modified version of the game while it is active.

Why Olympus?

Olympus is already one of the maps used in Apex's current Ranked rotation after the Marked Split 2 update. That makes the crossover particularly relevant because players familiar with the map will recognize its layout while encountering new presentation and event content. Respawn's September update placed Olympus alongside World's Edge and Broken Moon for the current ranked split.

How it fits into the current season

The Street Fighter 6 event arrives during Season 30: Marked, a season that has already introduced balance changes, loot adjustments, weapon buffs and additional anti-cheat measures. The September 14 midseason patch was followed by further bug fixes on September 17 and September 21.

That means the crossover is launching at the same time players are adapting to a new Ranked environment. Competitive players have a different set of priorities, while casual players can focus more on the limited-time content and cosmetics.

What players should know

The event launches September 22, so players who want to experience it should check the in-game event page when it goes live. Limited-time events can have their own availability windows and rewards, so the exact end date and item schedule should be checked through the game's official announcements.

For Apex fans, this is another example of the game's strategy of connecting its live service with major entertainment brands. The Street Fighter 6 crossover adds a distinctly fighting-game flavor to the battle royale while keeping the familiar Outlands setting and core gameplay loop.
