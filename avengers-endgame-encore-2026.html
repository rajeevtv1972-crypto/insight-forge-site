Avengers: Endgame Encore

The MCU classic returns to cinemas in September 2026

Avengers: Endgame is getting another theatrical run in September 2026. Disney's current movie listings show an Endgame Encore arriving in theaters on September 25, giving MCU fans another chance to experience the 2019 finale on the big screen.

Why the Encore?

Large-screen re-releases let studios bring major catalog titles back to cinemas around special events, anniversaries or new franchise milestones. For audiences, the appeal is simple: revisiting a major MCU moment with a theater crowd.

What to Remember Before Watching

The film closes several story threads established across the Infinity Saga and serves as a major transition point for the MCU. A refresher on Infinity War, the Avengers' earlier battles and the Infinity Stones can make the return even more rewarding.

What Comes After Endgame?

The timing also places the encore in an MCU landscape that includes Spider-Man: Brand New Day and the upcoming Avengers: Doomsday, which Marvel currently lists for December 18, 2026. That makes the theatrical return a useful reminder of how much the franchise has changed since Endgame.
