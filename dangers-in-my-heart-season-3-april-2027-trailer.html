The Dangers in My Heart has revealed a Season 3 teaser visual and trailer, confirming an April 2027 premiere and continuing Kyotaro and Anna’s story after Season 2.

Kyotaro and Anna’s story continues

The third season of The Dangers in My Heart, also known as BokuYaba, is scheduled to begin broadcasting in April 2027. The announcement arrived with a new teaser visual and promotional video, giving viewers an early look at the next phase of the relationship between Kyotaro Ichikawa and Anna Yamada.

The second season ended with the two becoming a couple. The new visual places them together on a park swing at sunset, carrying the tagline “A new world for me begins” and signalling a new stage for the central romance.

A teaser that hints at a wider cast

The promotional video also includes familiar classmates and family members, including Moeko Sekine and Kyotaro’s older sister Kana. It offers brief glimpses of the people around the central pair, suggesting their new relationship will affect the wider social circle too.

Studio Shin-Ei is again handling animation production, giving the sequel continuity with the earlier television seasons.

April is confirmed; the exact date is not

The official announcement confirms April 2027 but does not specify a day, episode count or a complete international streaming schedule. Viewers should treat the month-level window as the confirmed information for now.

Insight Forge takeaway: Season 3 is officially set for April 2027, with a teaser visual and trailer now available. The story picks up after Kyotaro and Anna become a couple, while the exact premiere day remains unannounced.
