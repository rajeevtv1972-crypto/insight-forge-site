Minecraft's Sift Dimension Revealed: The New World Coming in 2027

Minecraft's Sift is the first new dimension in more than 14 years and will move from Minecraft Dungeons II into the main game in 2027.

Minecraft has revealed something fans have been waiting for for more than a decade: a new dimension. Called the Sift, the new realm is being introduced through Minecraft Dungeons II and will later become part of the main Minecraft Java and Bedrock experience in 2027.

The first new dimension in over 14 years

Xbox Wire describes the Sift as Minecraft's first new dimension in more than 14 years. The main game has long revolved around the Overworld, Nether and End, so another realm changes the exploration map players have known for years.

What makes the Sift different?

The Sift has its own visual identity, new mobs and environmental rules. Xbox says players will encounter shifting conditions, hazardous blocks and new environments that affect exploration. Two named biomes have already been revealed: Meadows and Carapace, while additional areas remain secret.

Why Dungeons II comes first

Minecraft Dungeons II launches September 29 across Xbox Series X|S, Xbox on PC, Steam, PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2, according to Xbox Wire. It is also included with Game Pass and supports Xbox Play Anywhere.

Introducing the Sift through Dungeons II lets Mojang establish its creatures, environments and rules in a directed action-adventure setting before moving the concept into Minecraft's open-ended sandbox.

What could change in Minecraft?

In Java and Bedrock, players could eventually use the Sift for exploration, survival, resource gathering and building. Mojang has not yet revealed every access rule or resource system, so questions remain about how players enter the dimension and what unique materials will be available.

New building possibilities

New Minecraft environments quickly become building material. The Sift could introduce new blocks, colors and architectural themes, while its environmental rules may change how players travel and survive. The combination could create new survival strategies and building styles.

A two-stage reveal

Mojang is effectively giving players a preview through Dungeons II and a larger sandbox version later. Players who explore Dungeons II can learn about the Sift before its 2027 arrival in Java and Bedrock, giving the community time to understand its mobs, biomes and mechanics.

Final takeaway

The Sift is more than another Minecraft biome. It is a new dimension, the first major addition of its kind in more than 14 years. It arrives first in Minecraft Dungeons II on September 29 and is planned for the main Java and Bedrock editions in 2027, potentially opening a new chapter for Minecraft exploration.

Why the community will be watching closely

Minecraft updates become especially interesting when they introduce mechanics that can change how survival worlds are designed. A new dimension can affect exploration routes, resource economies, mob farms, transportation systems and multiplayer bases. The Sift could eventually become part of all of those systems once it reaches Java and Bedrock.

For now, Mojang is deliberately keeping some details secret. That means the community will likely learn about the dimension in stages, first through Dungeons II and later through the main game's own development and release information. The unanswered questions are part of the appeal: access rules, resources, mobs and building materials will determine how important the Sift becomes.

What builders and survival players should watch

The most important details for the main Minecraft release will eventually be practical ones. Players will want to know which Sift resources can be transported between dimensions, whether new blocks have special properties, how mobs interact with existing farms and whether the new dimension changes progression. Those answers will determine whether the Sift becomes an occasional destination or a permanent part of everyday survival worlds.
