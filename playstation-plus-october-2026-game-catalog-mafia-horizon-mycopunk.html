PlayStation Plus October 2026: Mafia, Horizon and Mycopunk Explained

The October lineup spans cinematic action, open-world exploration and co-op combat—here is how to choose what to play first.

Published October 16, 2026 · Insight Forge
HomeAnimeMoviesTechnologySpaceGamingLatestSearch

Sony’s October 14 PlayStation Blog post says the Game Catalog additions include Mafia: The Old Country, Horizon Forbidden West and Mycopunk, with the main lineup becoming available October 20 unless otherwise noted. PlayStation Plus Premium also gets Dino Crisis 2 and RollerCoaster Tycoon 3 Complete Edition. The appeal is variety: a cinematic crime story, a large exploration-focused adventure and a co-op shooter offer very different ways to spend the same subscription month.

Mafia: The Old Country for story-first players

Sony describes the game as a third-person action-adventure set in early twentieth-century Sicily, following Enzo Favara in a dangerous criminal underworld. Its historical setting and cinematic presentation make it the obvious choice for players who prefer missions and character drama over a huge checklist of side activities. If you are new to the series, treat it as a distinct entry and check the game page for platform and feature details.

Horizon Forbidden West for exploration

The return of Aloy offers a different pace: a frontier to explore, machine enemies to study and a story about a post-apocalyptic world facing new threats. Players who enjoy optional objectives and learning enemy patterns may prefer it to a shorter linear campaign. Because it is a re-release in the catalog, returning subscribers should confirm which edition and platform version is included in their regional service.

Mycopunk for co-op action

Sony describes Mycopunk as a mission-based looter shooter featuring a squad of robot rejects, customisable upgrades and four-player co-op. Its October 20 day-one catalog arrival may appeal to friends who want a shared session rather than a long solo narrative. As with any co-op game, the experience will depend on party size, communication and whether your group enjoys repeating missions to improve equipment.

Check the tier and the date

The blog post separates Game Catalog additions from Premium classics, and one title’s availability can differ from another’s. Open the official post and the console’s local PS Plus hub before making plans. Confirm the account’s tier, region and whether the desired game is included; a title mentioned in a monthly announcement may not be accessible to every subscriber.

A sensible first-night plan

Choose Mafia if the group wants a narrative, Horizon if you want to explore at your own pace, and Mycopunk if several friends can play together. Download sizes and patches may affect when a game is ready, so queue it ahead of time. The strongest lineup is not the one you sample all at once; it is the one that matches how you actually like to play.

Insight Forge takeaway: Choose Mafia if the group wants a narrative, Horizon if you want to explore at your own pace, and Mycopunk if several friends can play together.
