Mercedes-Benz India says it is adding ₹200 crore to local investment this year, taking cumulative investment to ₹3,200 crore, while exploring wider sourcing from Indian suppliers.

More investment in the Indian supplier ecosystem

Mercedes-Benz India is adding a further ₹200 crore to its investment in the country this year, taking its cumulative investment to approximately ₹3,200 crore, according to reporting by The Economic Times. The company’s focus includes expanding local sourcing and working more closely with Indian component suppliers.

The report says Mercedes-Benz sold 15,190 vehicles in India between January and September 2026, an increase of 8 per cent compared with the same period a year earlier.

Local sourcing is not the same as a new assembly commitment

The company’s stated direction is to deepen localisation of parts and procurement. However, the report notes that Mercedes-Benz has not made a decision to move additional models into a larger local manufacturing programme on a model-by-model basis.

More parts sourced from India can strengthen the supplier base without necessarily meaning that a new vehicle assembly line or locally manufactured model has been confirmed.

Why manufacturers are watching local content

India’s supplier ecosystem offers automakers opportunities to improve resilience, build regional capabilities and serve domestic and export requirements. Premium manufacturers must also balance local sourcing with quality, technical requirements and global platform standards.

The next indicators to watch are new supplier partnerships, the components selected for localisation and any future announcement about vehicle assembly. For now, the confirmed story is a deeper sourcing effort backed by additional investment.

Insight Forge takeaway: Mercedes-Benz India is adding ₹200 crore to its local investment this year, bringing its cumulative total to ₹3,200 crore. Wider sourcing is the stated priority; no new model-by-model manufacturing decision was confirmed.
