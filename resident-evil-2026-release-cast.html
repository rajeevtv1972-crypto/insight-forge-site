Resident Evil (2026)

Release date, cast and the new survival-horror story

HomeAnimeMoviesTechSpaceGaming

Resident Evil is scheduled to hit theaters on September 18, 2026. Sony Pictures describes it as an all-new story and a reinvention of the franchise rather than a direct retelling of one game.

The New Story

The film follows Bryan, a medical courier whose ordinary night turns into a race for survival. Sony describes the story as a chaotic descent into a nightmare, giving the movie a fresh entry point for viewers who may know the games as well as newcomers.

Cast & Director

The film is directed by Zach Cregger, with a cast led by Austin Abrams, Zach Cherry, Kali Reis and Paul Walter Hauser. Cregger also co-wrote the screenplay with Shay Hatten.

Why Horror Fans Are Watching

The movie is arriving at a time when major game adaptations have become a regular part of cinema. Its separate storyline gives the filmmakers room to establish their own tone while still using the survival-horror identity associated with Resident Evil.

Release

Sony Pictures lists the film for an exclusive theatrical release on September 18, 2026. International schedules may vary by market.
