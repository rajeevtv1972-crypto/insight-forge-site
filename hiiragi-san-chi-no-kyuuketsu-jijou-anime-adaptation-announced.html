A new vampire-themed romantic comedy is heading to television. TMS Entertainment announced on October 31, 2026 that Miki Yoshikawa’s manga Hiiragi-san Chi no Kyuuketsu Jijou will receive a TV anime adaptation. The announcement arrived with a new teaser visual featuring Yukieda, the second of the three vampire sisters in the Hiiragi family.

The manga follows Shota Minamoto, a middle-school student living in a children’s home who is taken in by the Hiiragi family. He soon discovers that the household is made up of vampires, and his new life involves living alongside the family’s three sisters. TMS describes the premise as a home romantic comedy with a darker, gothic atmosphere.

What the first visual tells us

The teaser visual focuses on Yukieda approaching Shota, leaning into the contrast between a cute character design and the supernatural premise. The original creator has also commented on the adaptation, highlighting the story’s contrast between bright daytime settings and a carefully designed night-time atmosphere. That visual contrast could be an important part of the anime’s identity.

What has not been announced yet

TMS has confirmed the adaptation and released the first promotional visual, but it has not yet announced a broadcast season, premiere date, main voice cast or episode count. Those details should be treated as pending. A teaser visual is an early announcement asset, not evidence that the series is close to airing.

For fans of Yoshikawa’s work, the adaptation is notable because it brings a newer manga property to television. The next updates to watch are the animation staff, cast reveal and the first proper promotional video.
